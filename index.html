Pathlight Community Services is working in partnership with Lenawee Community Mental Health Authority to transition service provision at the Lenawee County locations currently operated by Christ Centered Homes (CCH). Although we are in the very early stages of this transition, we want to reassure you that current CCH employees will play an important role in ensuring continuity of care and maintaining the quality of services that individuals and families have come to know and rely upon.

Christ Centered Homes has a long-standing history of dedicated, compassionate, and skilled caregivers. We recognize and value the experience, knowledge, and relationships you have developed with the individuals you serve. You are welcome and encouraged to apply for employment with Pathlight Community Services as we move forward with this transition.

Our goal is to retain current caregivers and, whenever possible, maintain staffing within the programs and locations where you currently work. We believe that retaining familiar caregivers will help minimize disruption, promote stability, and ensure the individuals we collectively serve continue receiving consistent, high-quality care. We appreciate your dedication to the individuals in your care and hope you will consider continuing that important work with Pathlight Community Services.
